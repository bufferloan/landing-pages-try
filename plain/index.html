What this is, plainly.

We cover a customer's first car payment. 0%, no fees, nothing owed at signing. The customer never pays us anything and never pays interest.

Why a dealer would want that:

  • Lenders track early payment default at the dealer level.
  • A customer who misses inside the first three payments counts against your store, not against them.
  • Your rating with the lender sets your advance. Whatever the lender will not advance, the customer has to cover in cash.
  • Keep those early payments current and the advances loosen.
